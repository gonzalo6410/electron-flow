What is new since the last review (all of it is yours to review): curved arrows and moves.
- core/moves.js: an arrow moves one electron pair (tail: a lone pair or a bond; head: an atom or two atoms). applyArrows applies a set of arrows to one Lewis structure and refuses with a plain reason (a pair that is not there, an atom over its ceiling, a hydrogen left with nothing). commit classifies the result: resonance (the result is one of the node's own contributing structures: nothing to commit), a move (a sigma bond made or broken: a new node, with each piece keeping its electrons and loose ions tied again), or refused (also when no bond changes and the picture is not a contributing structure). specAfter builds the new node; transitionFrames gives the hybrid at a run of mixes; combine puts two molecules in one scene.
- core/reactions.js: reaction scenes (SN2, SN1 in three steps, E2, acid and base, enolate, aldol, Michael, Diels Alder) with their textbook arrows named by SMILES position, and pieceFormulas.
- web/arrows.js: drawing arrows with the pointer: drag from a pair of dots or from a bond to an atom or a bond; the arrow bows away from the molecule; Enter or the button makes the move; Escape clears; a click on an arrow removes it; red halos on the atoms a refusal names.
- web/app.js: the wiring (search for "moves:"): makeMove, playMove and stepMovie (the film: approach, blend of the weights with the shape relaxed at 21 mixes, settle; then the products are loaded), the reaction scenes list with "show the arrows", adding a compound beside from the search box, invisible pick lines along every bond, fading ion ties during the film.
- core/geometry.js bondLength and springTerms: a bond being made or broken is long and weak at low order so the film shows the atoms drifting together.
- tests: test/moves.js, test/reactions.js. Run everything: node test/run.js, families, pi, edit, smiles, compounds, shapes, gradient, extra, rdkit-agreed, geometry, moves, reactions.
